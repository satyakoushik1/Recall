QUESTIONS

Fair questions.

What is Recall? +
Recall is a study companion that reads your uploaded PDF notes and answers questions directly from them, with the exact source cited — then offers to quiz you on it.
Is my data private? +
Yes. Your uploads are private to your account by default, and are only used to answer your questions and generate your practice material.
What does it cost? +
Free to start — creating an account and generating your first decks costs nothing. Paid plans unlock unlimited uploads and exam-mode feedback.
Which file types work? +
Currently PDF only — export your notes, slides, or scans to PDF before uploading. More formats are on the roadmap.
Do I need my own AI account? +
No. Recall handles the AI behind the scenes, so there's nothing extra to sign up for or configure.
Does Recall replace my textbook? +
No — it works alongside your existing material, helping you retrieve and practice what's already in it, grounded in your own notes.
